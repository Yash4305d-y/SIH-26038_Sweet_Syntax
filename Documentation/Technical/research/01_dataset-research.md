# Dataset Research


1. APTOS 2019 — DR grading
APTOS explicitly contains variable-quality images, including blur, underexposure and overexposure, which is useful for your image-quality research.

   https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/aptos2019-blindness-detection/data?utm_source=chatgpt.com

2. IDRiD
It is especially valuable for your segmentation + lesion-detection + explainability work.

   https://idrid.grand-challenge.org/Data/?utm_source=chatgpt.com

3. DRIVE — Vessel segmentation
Use this specifically for retinal blood-vessel segmentation.

   https://drive.grand-challenge.org/DRIVE/?utm_source=chatgpt.com

5. Messidor-2 — External validation
It contains 1,748 images from 874 examinations. Note that the official Messidor-2 database itself does not provide a DR ground-truth annotation; third-party annotations are separate. Download requires completing their form.

   https://www.adcis.net/en/third-party/messidor2/?utm_source=chatgpt.com
