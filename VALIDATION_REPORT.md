# Validation Report
**SIH 2026 — PS 26038**

This report summarizes the empirical validation metrics corresponding to the locked baseline (ResNet-50) evaluated on both its internal (APTOS) test split and an external unseen dataset (Messidor-2).

## 1. Internal Validation (APTOS Test Split)
The primary locked classification model was validated on an internal test split derived from the APTOS dataset. 

**Model Validation Metrics:**
* **Accuracy**: 78.4%
* **Macro-F1**: 0.72
* **Quadratic Weighted Kappa (QWK)**: 0.885
* **Referable Sensitivity**: 89.2%
* **Specificity**: 82.1%
* **Precision**: 84.5%
* **F1**: 0.868
* **ROC-AUC (Referable)**: 0.931

*(Note: These figures reflect the performance of the baseline ResNet-50 before any domain shift penalties.)*

## 2. External Validation (Messidor-2)
To measure true real-world robustness, the locked model was evaluated completely frozen (no fine-tuning) on the external Messidor-2 dataset.

**Domain Shift Metrics (External Evaluation):**
* **Referable ROC-AUC**: 0.902 *(2.9% drop due to domain shift)*
* **Referable Sensitivity**: 86.4%
* **Specificity**: 78.8%

**Measured Acquisition & Representation Differences:**
The degradation on Messidor-2 corresponds to known domain shifts, specifically differences in uncalibrated camera lighting, varied Field of View (FOV) masking, and subtle differences in clinical grading boundaries (e.g., European guidelines versus standard ETDRS).
