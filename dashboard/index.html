19. Limitations & Responsible Use

  • APTOS-trained performance does not guarantee performance on every real-world retinal camera/domain.
  • Messidor-2 demonstrated domain-generalization limitations.
  • Grade 3 and Grade 4 recall are weaker than Grade 0/2.
  • Grad-CAM demonstrates model attention, not clinical proof.
  • The system is a screening/decision-support prototype, not an autonomous diagnosis system.
  • Uncertain or unsuitable images should be reviewed/re-captured according to the workflow.
  • Broader multi-dataset clinical validation is required before real clinical deployment.
